01 — Overview

A year building the visual identity for UW's Tea Club — across print, social, and video.

I grew the community by 600+ followers, designing event posters, stickers, and weekly content while collaborating with other campus clubs to create a distinctive, recognisable brand presence.

02

What I Did

Six areas of work across the full brand lifecycle — from social content to physical print collateral.

Weekly Content & Growth

Managed weekly Instagram posts. Grew follower count by 600+ using Blender 3D assets and custom colour gradients.

Cross-Club Events

Partnered with Cheese Club and DJ Club for joint events. Curated collaborative playlists to extend the brand beyond visual design.

Reels & Video

Produced and edited short-form video for Instagram Reels and stories, shot and cut in-house using Adobe Premiere Pro.

Posters & Stickers

Designed 100–200 copies of event posters and pixel art stickers. So well-received other club booths inquired about the design.

Visual Identity

Evolved the brand visual language over a year, experimenting with styles and palettes refined on community feedback.

Website Assets

Designed website components in Figma and collaborated with executives to bring them to life in HTML / CSS.
