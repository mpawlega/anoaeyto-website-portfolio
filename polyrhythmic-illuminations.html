02 — Concept & System

Rhythm made visible.

Phil Kline's Unsilent Night layers five independent rhythmic voices — each at a prime ratio to the others — producing a counterpoint that never fully resolves. Each voice is given a physical LED presence, making visible what is ordinarily only heard.
